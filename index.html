How a refurbishment works

You hand over a tired dispenser. You get back one that looks clean and runs properly.

  1. InspectWe check the cooling, heating, taps and body for faults.
  2. Strip and cleanWe take it apart and sanitise every part that touches water.
  3. Replace partsWorn taps, thermostats and seals are replaced, not patched.
  4. Test and returnWe run it through full cooling and heating cycles before delivery.

Fresh filters, better-tasting water.

Filters wear out, and old ones can leave water tasting flat or smelling off. We replace them for you and leave the dispenser clean.

Signs it is time for a filter service

The water tastes or smells different.

The flow from the tap has slowed down.

It has been about six months since the last change, or longer.
